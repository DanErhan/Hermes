WHAT YOU BRING — AND WHY THAT'S THE POINT

Your broker account. Hermes connects to your own cTrader account, demo or live. It never holds your funds and there is nothing custodial anywhere in the system.

Your AI keys. Voice and reasoning run on your own provider accounts — your usage, your control. Skip them and the core system still works.

Questions a careful tester asks.

Start on a demo account or a live one?

Demo, for at least the first two weeks. You will learn everything you need to about how Hermes decides and manages trades without a single euro at risk, and if something about it does not suit you, you will find that out for free. Move to live, small, once it has earned it.

Is this a signal service or a managed account?

Neither. Hermes is software that runs on your machine against your own broker account. Nobody trades for you, nobody pools funds, and no signals are sent to anyone. You configure the risk envelope; Hermes operates inside it.

What win rate should I expect?

Hermes doesn't promise one — and treats systems that do as a warning sign. Its discipline is structural: evidence-gated entries, clocked position protection, and execution review that only claims improvement when out-of-sample data shows it. Trading involves real risk of loss, with or without good software.

Does my trading data leave my machine?

No. The journal, broker credentials and API keys are stored encrypted on your hardware, keyed through your OS keychain. The licence server receives your email and a salted machine fingerprint for activation — it never sees trades, balances or keys.

What happens when testing ends?

You will be told well before anything changes, and nothing will be taken away without warning. What Hermes becomes after this stage is not decided yet, and it will be decided partly by what this group finds.

Which brokers work?

cTrader today (demo and live), with MetaTrader support in active development. If your broker offers cTrader, Hermes can trade it.

Hermes is trading software, not investment advice, and past performance — the system's or yours — does not guarantee future results. Trading leveraged products carries a real risk of loss; never trade money you cannot afford to lose. You remain responsible for your account, your configuration and your risk.
