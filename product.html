HOW HERMES WORKS

A trading system that has to
show its evidence.

Hermes runs on your machine, against your broker account. It analyzes markets, selects and skips trades, manages every open position on a fixed clock, journals everything, reviews its own execution — and is forbidden, by construction, from claiming anything its record can't back.

I · THE ENTRY GATE

Most of trading is refusing to trade.

Every signal passes a deterministic gate before it may become an order. The gate's rules are not opinions — each one entered service only after being measured on the real trade record, in-sample and out-of-sample. A rule that fails validation runs in observation, gathering evidence; it never blocks a trade on a hunch.

OBSERVE the market→ FILTER the setup→ CONFIRM the conditions→ ENTER or SKIP — on record, with the reason
FRESHNESS

No machine-gunning

A new trade requires a genuinely new opportunity. Re-entering the same market minutes after the last entry measured consistently worse across the whole record — so the gate refuses it, and says so in plain language.

DISCIPLINE

Every skip is accountable

Skipped signals are recorded with their reason. You can ask Hermes why it didn't trade, and the answer comes from the gate's own log — not a rationalization after the fact.

II · PROTECTION

Positions are managed on a clock,
not on attention.

Every open position is re-examined every few seconds — not when someone happens to look. Management is a ladder of deterministic rules, each one earned from replaying the account's own history with costs included.

BANK THE MOVE

Profit ladder

As a trade moves into profit, the stop follows in defined steps — first to break-even, then locking gains. A green trade is not allowed to quietly die red.

RECOVERY

Adverse-recovery protection

A trade that went against you and fought back to entry is a warning, not a victory. Hermes arms protection on the way down and moves the stop to entry on recovery — a pattern that saved multiples of what it cost, measured across hundreds of replayed trades.

EXECUTABLE STOPS

Spread-aware sizing

A stop inside the spread is not a stop — it's an oversized position with no exit. Stops are floored against the instrument's spread before sizing, so position size can never explode on a microscopic stop distance.

III · EXECUTION INTELLIGENCE

It grades its own execution,
then fixes it.

Every closed trade is reconstructed from the broker's own minute bars: how far it went against you, how far it went in your favor, whether the opportunity was real and whether the execution captured it. Trades are classified as successful or failed executions — and failures feed a knowledge base that graduates from logged → monitoring → corrected as the evidence accumulates.

FORENSICS

Per-trade anatomy

Entry, stop, the price path, the protection timeline, and a plain-language narrative: why it was taken, what happened, how it was managed, and the verdict. No debug logs, no jargon — the anatomy of the trade.

THE LOOP

Lessons become rules

A recurring failure pattern doesn't become a lecture — it becomes a candidate rule, validated against held-out history, and only then deployed to the entry gate or the protection ladder. If the data refuses the rule, the rule is refused.

IV · THE JOURNAL

Journaling with zero discipline required.

Every trade is journaled the moment it closes — entry, exit, context, money, and its execution verdict. The journal updates itself within minutes, lives encrypted on your machine, and is the ground truth every answer is computed from. Ask about last month and the answer is arithmetic, not memory.

V · THE VOICE

A desk assistant, not a chatbot.

Press one key and talk to your desk. Questions about your record are answered from the record. Opinions come with reasoning. And trade instructions run through a deterministic grammar — parsed, prepared, shown back to you — so a language model can never invent an order.

“How did I do on gold this month?”
Computed from the journal — the numbers, and what they mean.
“Do you think that was a good trade?”
A reasoned judgement against your own evidence — not a data dump.
“Short gold, one percent risk, forty pip stop.”
Parsed deterministically, prepared as a ticket, executed on your word.
VI · AUTONOMY

You decide how much rope it gets.

SIMULATION

Watch it work

Hermes trades a paper book. You watch the decisions, the skips, and the journal fill — risking nothing.

CONFIRMATION

It proposes, you dispose

Every entry is prepared and shown. Nothing fires without your word.

AUTONOMOUS

The full desk

Hermes trades within the risk envelope you configured, on its own clock, with every decision on the record.

VII · THE HONESTY RULES

What Hermes will never tell you.

  • That profits are guaranteed, or a win rate is promised.
  • That a trade was placed, unless the broker confirmed the fill.
  • That a strategy “works”, when its out-of-sample evidence says otherwise.
  • That an improvement happened, when the data shows none.

These are not marketing lines — they are constraints in the software, each one guarded by its own regression test. Trading involves real risk of loss. Hermes is an instrument of discipline, not a promise of profit.
